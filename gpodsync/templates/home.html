gpodsync

A small, self-hosted podcast sync server, compatible with the gpodder.net API and built to work — reliably — with AntennaPod. Your subscriptions and listening positions, on every device, on your own machine.

Connect AntennaPod

  1. Open Settings → Synchronization and choose gpodder.net as the provider.
  2. Set the server to this host
  3. Log in with the account your administrator created, pick a device name, and sync.

There is nothing to browse here: this server has no web interface, no public registration and no podcast directory. It speaks to your podcast app, and to nothing else.

What it keeps in sync

Subscriptions
Shared across all of an account's devices — a fresh install receives the full list.
Episodes
Listening positions and played state, resolved so the newest progress wins.
Your data
Stays on this server. Feeds are never fetched, visits are not tracked.
